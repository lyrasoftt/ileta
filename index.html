Ileta — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ileta application (“Ileta”, “the application”) handles information in connection with your use of it. Ileta is developed and published by Lyrasoft (“we”, “us”, “our”).

1. Information we collect

Ileta does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The application has no user accounts, no authentication, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. All circuits, saved projects, and preferences remain solely on your device within the application’s private storage and are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Bug reports. The “Report a bug” item in the menu opens your own email application with a message addressed to us. The message is pre-filled with the application version, the Android version and the device model, and the circuit currently on screen is attached so we can reproduce the problem. Nothing is sent automatically: you can read and edit the message, remove the attachment, or discard it. If you send it, we receive your email address and the content of the message, and use them only to investigate the problem and to reply to you. We do not share bug reports with third parties and delete them once the issue is resolved.

2. Network communications

Ileta does not transmit personal data. Network use is limited to the following:

  • Google Play Billing. When you open the “Unlock Features” screen or complete a purchase, the application communicates with Google Play Billing. This communication occurs directly between your device and Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. Ileta does not receive or process your payment details.
  • Configuration retrieval. On startup, the application performs a single anonymous HTTPS request to retrieve a small configuration file that governs an optional promotional trial. This request transmits no account information, device identifier, or other personal data; it only downloads the file.

3. Permissions

Ileta declares the INTERNET and network-state permissions solely for the purposes described in Section 2. It requests no permissions that require your consent at runtime. Importing or exporting a circuit uses the Android system file picker, which grants the application access only to the specific file you select.

4. Children’s privacy

Ileta is an educational application intended for a general audience. It displays no advertising, provides no user-generated content or communication features, and collects no personal information from users of any age, apart from the optional bug report described in Section 1, which is only sent if the user chooses to send it.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected by the “Effective date” above, and the current version will always be the one linked from the application’s Google Play store listing.

6. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Lyrasoft at lyrasoftsupport@gmail.com.
